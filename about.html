About me

As a GIS Technician for the City of Lawrence, I help maintain underground water utility assets, perform spatial data collection with GPS, and clean transportation data using Python and Excel. Previously, I served as a GIS Intern for the Johnson County Parks and Recreation District (JCPRD), where I conducted spatial suitability and site selection analyses, digitized satellite imagery for park infrastructure, and mapped culverts using ArcGIS Field Maps and Trimble GPS units. Before transitioning into municipal GIS, I worked as a Weekend Meteorologist at News-Press NOW and a Weather Observations Recorder at the University of Kansas, where I tracked radar data, analyzed multi-sensor feeds, and reported climate data to the NWS and NOAA.

In May 2026, I graduated from the University of Kansas receiving a B.S. in Geography with a Certificate in Geographic Information Science (GIS). While pursuing my degree, I worked as a Weather Observations Recorder and served as Vice President of the KU Student Chapter of the American Meteorological Society, managing chapter finances and organizing industry networking events. My undergraduate research focused on modeling KC metro housing affordability trends (2018–2023) using ACS data, Zillow housing values, and regression models built in R.

I hope to continue my career in geospatial analysis, data science, and urban planning. I am also passionate about doing research relating to spatial data analysis, environmental monitoring, climate change, and geospatial problem-solving. My goal is to increase skills in programming and utilize technology to better understand our environment and communities.
